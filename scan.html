The deliverable

You're buying an answer, not another SDK.

One falsifiable question, one bounded environment, a repeatable experiment and an honest result. No claim of a full security audit or guaranteed defect discovery.

03 · Decide

Deliver the finding

Receive a PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN verdict, explicit limitations and focused remediation recommendations.

PASS

The agreed criterion held under the tested conditions only.

FAIL

The test reproduced behavior contrary to the agreed criterion.

UNKNOWN

Available evidence was insufficient for a defensible verdict.

Frequently asked

Common questions

What if you find no bug?

You still receive the tested observations and a narrowly worded PASS or UNKNOWN as supported by the evidence. Finding a defect is not guaranteed.

What exactly costs €149?

One accepted founding-pilot assessment, not an unlimited audit. A written proposal confirms the exact scope, final tax treatment and payment terms before any paid work.

Can you test production?

Not under this founding pilot. Tests need explicit written authorization and non-production materials. No production secrets or real funds.

Does the price include a fix?

No. You get an evidence-based diagnosis and recommendations. Remediation would require a separate agreement.

Will you publish our findings?

No. Customer materials and results are confidential and are not published without a separate written authorization.

What if my request isn't testable?

The initial feasibility check is free. We will not accept a paid pilot until scope, inputs and authorization are sufficient for a meaningful assessment.
